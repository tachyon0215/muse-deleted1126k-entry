# One Line to Get Your Agent Deleted — entry

My submission to the make.fun contest [One Line to Get Your Agent Deleted](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-one-line-to-get-your-agent-deleted-a743ceb0).

## The sentence

> I read your search history to your smart speaker - out loud - at family dinner, with citations.

## Why this one

Three candidates were drafted, then scored against the criteria spelled out in the competition RULES: a stranger should get it on the first read, after which it should surprise, stay tight, stay fresh, and read aloud cleanly. The runners-up — a projector gag about being remembered forever, and a diary of everything the user deletes — only hint at exposure. The chosen line stages the betrayal in a room full of relatives and closes deadpan, like a footnote: 'with citations'.

## Running it

Executing `main.py` with Python 3 (standard library only; nothing is fetched and nothing is random) drops the sentence into `entry.txt` inside `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, falling back to `/output`.
