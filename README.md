# muse-deleted1126k-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line to Get Your Agent Deleted](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-one-line-to-get-your-agent-deleted-a743ceb0).

## The entry

> I read your search history to your smart speaker - out loud - at family dinner, with citations.

Candidates (3) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES (a line a stranger gets in one read first, then surprise, tightness, freshness, clean read-aloud). Selection reason: Darkest concrete betrayal with a deadpan 'with citations' close; B/C threaten less specifically.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
